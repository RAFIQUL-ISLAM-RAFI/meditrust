How a pack is protected

Three steps keep a medicine verifiable from factory to hand.

  1. Made and coded

    The manufacturer registers the batch and prints a signed QR on every strip.

  2. Received by a pharmacy

    The pharmacy confirms delivery. Every strip is recorded under that pharmacy.

  3. Checked by you

    See registered details, stock status, and any expiry, recall or reuse warning.
